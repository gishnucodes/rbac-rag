# HR Compensation Bands & Compensation Structure

This confidential reference document details the active compensation bands, salary ranges, equity benchmarks, and variable compensation guidelines across job families and seniority levels at Acme Corp.

## Overview & Compensation Methodology

Acme Corp utilizes a standardized level hierarchy (L3 through L8) to evaluate job complexity, scope of operational responsibility, and market competitiveness.

* **Market Benchmarking:** Compensation bands are reviewed annually against top-tier industry salary survey data for technology companies and corporate service benchmarks.
* **Geographic Tiers:** Compensation figures in this document represent Tier 1 national base locations (e.g. major metropolitan technology hubs). Tier 2 and Tier 3 adjustments apply according to standard regional policy discounts (90% and 82% respectively).
* **Pay Parity & Equity:** Annual audits are conducted by HR leadership to ensure pay equity across demographic groups and performance cohorts.

## Engineering & Technology Compensation Bands

The table below reflects total target compensation ranges, including base salary, target performance bonus percentages, and annualized equity grants.

* **L3 Software Engineer:** Base range $112,000 – $138,000; Target performance bonus 8%; Initial equity grant $25,000 vesting over 4 years.
* **L4 Senior Software Engineer:** Base range $145,000 – $172,000; Target performance bonus 12%; Initial equity grant $60,000 vesting over 4 years.
* **L5 Staff Engineer:** Base range $187,400 – $220,000; Target performance bonus 15%; Initial equity grant $120,000 vesting over 4 years. Reference control index COMPBAND-9921.
* **L6 Principal Engineer:** Base range $230,000 – $275,000; Target performance bonus 20%; Initial equity grant $220,000 vesting over 4 years.

## Corporate & Management Compensation Bands

Management and corporate functional roles align with equivalent engineering level pay bands:

* **L5 Manager / Lead:** Base range $180,000 – $215,000; Target performance bonus 15%.
* **L6 Director:** Base range $225,000 – $265,000; Target performance bonus 25%.
* **L7 Senior Director / VP:** Base range $280,000 – $340,000; Target performance bonus 35%.

## Promotion & Out-of-Cycle Adjustments

Out-of-cycle salary adjustments or compensation band overrides require written justification from the department head and explicit approval from HR Leadership and Executive Committee. All salary adjustments take effect on the first day of the pay period following approval.
