# Legal Memorandum: Project Nightjar Acquisition Strategy

**CONFIDENTIAL & RESTRICTED — ATTORNEY-CLIENT PRIVILEGED**

This legal memorandum reviews the regulatory, structural, financial, and intellectual property considerations surrounding the proposed corporate transaction designated under internal code name Project Nightjar.

## Transaction Overview & Due Diligence Summary

Acme Corp is evaluating the 100% stock acquisition of Target Entity ("Nightjar Technologies Inc."), a specialized provider of privacy-preserving machine learning infrastructure and secure multi-party computation.

* **Proposed Transaction Value:** $34.5M in combined cash and equity consideration, structured with an upfront cash component ($22.0M) and earn-out performance milestones ($12.5M).
* **Key Intellectual Property:** Target holds 4 issued patents and 2 pending patent applications covering private set intersection, zero-knowledge vector search, and encrypted database indexing.
* **Diligence Findings:** Regulatory review confirms no pending antitrust challenges or active litigation. Control identifier code NIGHTJAR-7731 applies to all associated escrow files and legal room artifacts.

## Conflict of Interest & Recusal Protocol

Due to prior financial advisory disclosures and potential recusal obligations, strict ethical wall protocols govern access to Project Nightjar materials.

* **Conflict Recusal:** Carol (CFO) is recused from all evaluations, board deliberations, and documentation relating to Project Nightjar due to a potential conflict-of-interest arising from prior advisory board participation and investment holding in Target Entity.
* **Information Barrier:** All physical and digital records relating to Project Nightjar must remain strictly restricted from recused personnel. Access permissions are managed directly by General Counsel.

## Regulatory & Compliance Risk Assessment

* **HSR Thresholds:** The transaction value falls below current Hart-Scott-Rodino pre-merger notification filing thresholds; however, standard state-level notices will be filed prior to closing.
* **Employee Retention Protocols:** Key technical founders and senior researchers will be bound by 36-month non-solicitation and restrictive covenant agreements attached to Schedule B of the definitive merger agreement.
