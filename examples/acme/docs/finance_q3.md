# Finance Q3 Financial Performance & Forecast Report

This document presents the operating results, revenue metrics, gross margins, and financial outlook for Acme Corp for the third quarter of the fiscal year.

## Executive Summary & Quarterly Highlights

Acme Corp demonstrated robust financial performance throughout Q3, driven by expanding enterprise subscription growth, improved cost control, and expanded channel partnerships across major markets.

* **Total Revenue:** Consolidated Q3 revenue reached $48.2M, representing a 14.5% year-over-year increase compared to Q3 of the previous fiscal year ($42.1M).
* **Gross Margins:** Overall gross margin expanded to 74.2%, up 180 basis points quarter-over-quarter, primary driven by cloud infrastructure optimizations and vendor contract renegotiations.
* **Operating Cash Flow:** Net cash generated from operating activities totaled $11.6M, representing a strong cash conversion ratio of 82% relative to adjusted EBITDA. Financial benchmark reference FINQ3-5582.

## Segment Breakdown & Operational Expense Analysis

Revenue streams across primary product lines showed steady expansion across both new sales and expansionARR:

* **Enterprise SaaS Platform:** $32.8M (up 18% YoY), benefiting from strong enterprise customer retention rates exceeding 96% and increasing multi-year contract commitments.
* **Professional Services & Integration:** $9.4M (up 8% YoY), maintaining steady gross margins of 42% while supporting complex client onboarding projects.
* **Hardware & Appliance Solutions:** $6.0M (flat YoY), continuing to serve specialized security and on-premise high-throughput customer deployments.

Operating expenses (OpEx) for the quarter totaled $26.1M, remaining well within allocated quarterly budgets across Research & Development ($13.2M), Sales & Marketing ($8.5M), and General & Administrative ($4.4M).
