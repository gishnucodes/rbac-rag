# Acme Corp Employee Handbook

Welcome to Acme Corp! This handbook outlines general workplace policies, procedures, and resources available to all team members, including full-time employees, contractors, and interns.

## Paid Time Off (PTO) Policy

At Acme Corp, we believe that taking time away from work is essential for health, well-being, and sustained productivity.

* **Annual Accrual:** Full-time team members accrue 25 days of paid time off per calendar year. PTO accrues on a bi-weekly basis starting from your first day of employment.
* **Rollover Limits:** A maximum of 5 unused PTO days may be carried over into the following calendar year. Any additional accrued PTO above 5 days must be used by December 31 or it will expire.
* **Scheduling PTO:** Requests for 3 or more consecutive days off should be submitted to your primary manager at least two weeks in advance using the HR portal.
* **Sick Leaves & Wellness Days:** PTO covers both personal vacation and non-emergency illness. For extended medical leaves, please consult the medical leave policy.

## Office Hours & Work Expectations

Acme Corp supports flexible working hours while maintaining core collaboration windows across global teams.

* **Core Hours:** Our primary operating hours are 09:00 to 17:00 local time. Core collaboration hours when meetings are typically scheduled are 10:00 to 15:00.
* **Remote & Hybrid Work:** Team members are encouraged to align their hybrid schedule with their project lead and local department norms.
* **Time Tracking:** Contractors and hourly employees should submit weekly timesheets via the online portal by end-of-day Friday.

## Visitor & Guest Wi-Fi Access

We offer secure internet access for guests, clients, and personal devices across all corporate campus locations.

* **Network Name (SSID):** `Acme-Guest`
* **Access Credentials:** The guest network password changes on the first Monday of every month. The current password can be obtained from the front desk reception or by typing `/wifi` in Slack.
* **Usage Policy:** Guest Wi-Fi is intended for web browsing, communication, and basic access. Streaming high-bandwidth non-work content is restricted.

## Expense Reimbursement Basics

Acme Corp reimburses reasonable and necessary expenses incurred while performing official business.

* **Filing Receipts:** All expense claims over $25 require an itemized receipt uploaded to the expense tracking system within 30 days of the transaction.
* **Travel Allowances:** Business travel must be pre-approved by your manager. Meal allowances while traveling on business are capped at $75 per day.
* **Hardware & Software Purchases:** Personal equipment or recurring software subscriptions must be routed through IT Procurement prior to purchase.
